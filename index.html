01 — What we believe

Four cuts.
Not forty.

A short list is a form of respect. Every cut we sell is named in plain language and is there for a reason, so there is no catalogue to scroll and no jargon to decode. Just chicken you can plan your week around.

04 — What makes it different

Small on purpose.
Plain on the label.

Words you won’t find on our packs

  • premium-grade
  • super-select
  • chef’s choice
  • ultra-fresh

Boneless Thighs. Said plainly.

  1. I

    A small range

    Four cuts means no wandering the aisle. You always know what you are ordering and why it is on the list.

  2. II

    Honest labelling

    Cut name first. Bone-in or boneless, said plainly. A label should tell you what is inside, not how to feel about it.

  3. III

    Fresh, on purpose

    Fresh chicken is the whole product, and it is why the range is small enough to do properly.
