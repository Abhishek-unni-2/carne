Our Story

More Than a Product.
A Point of View.

Carne & Gusto is a fresh chicken brand built on a point of view rather than a long product list. We believe everyday food deserves the same consideration as a special occasion — that quality should be quiet, consistent and within reach. So we keep the range deliberate: four cuts, and every one of them given our full attention. Fewer things, chosen carefully, described honestly.

  1. 01

    Product First

    What goes into the range matters more than how many things are in it. We would rather do a few things properly than many things adequately.

  2. 02

    Built on Trust

    Plain language, honest descriptions, no overstatement. You should always know exactly what you are bringing home and why it is worth it.

  3. 03

    Made for Everyday Life

    Good food should not be reserved for occasions. It should fit the way you actually cook, on the days you actually cook.
